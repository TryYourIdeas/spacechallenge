1. temperatura_terra.csv  -  Global 2 m air temperature (T2M)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Dataset:  NASA POWER, MERRA-2 Temperature at 2 Meters (C)
  Product:  Monthly and annual climatology, Jan 2001 - Dec 2025 (average, no year column)
  Grid:     0.5 deg lat x 0.625 deg lon, global, 207,936 cells
  Home:     https://power.larc.nasa.gov/
  Docs:     https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/
  API used: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/climatology/regional?parameters=T2M&community=RE&latitude-min=<lat>&latitude-max=<lat+10>&longitude-min=<lon>&longitude-max=<lon+10>&format=CSV
            (648 tiles of 10x10 deg, merged)
  Access:   no registration

2. precipitacao_terra.csv  -  Global precipitation (PRECTOTCORR)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Dataset:  NASA POWER, MERRA-2 Precipitation Corrected (mm/day)
  Product:  Monthly and annual climatology, Jan 2001 - Dec 2025 (average, no year column)
  Grid:     0.5 deg lat x 0.625 deg lon, global, 207,936 cells
  Home:     https://power.larc.nasa.gov/
  Docs:     https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/
  API used: https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/climatology/regional?parameters=PRECTOTCORR&community=RE&latitude-min=<lat>&latitude-max=<lat+10>&longitude-min=<lon>&longitude-max=<lon+10>&format=CSV
            (648 tiles of 10x10 deg, merged)
  Access:   no registration

3. permafrost_circumarctic_v2/  -  Permafrost and ground ice
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Dataset:  Circum-Arctic Map of Permafrost and Ground-Ice Conditions, Version 2
            (NSIDC GGD318.002; Brown et al.)
  Coverage: Northern Hemisphere, about 25N to 90N
  Landing:  https://nsidc.org/data/ggd318/versions/2
  DOI:      https://doi.org/10.7265/skbg-kf16
  User guide: https://nsidc.org/sites/default/files/ggd318-v002-userguide.pdf
  Files:    ftp://sidads.colorado.edu/pub/DATASETS/fgdc/ggd318_map_circumarctic/
            (shapefiles permaice/subsea/treeline, EASE-Grid nhipa/nlipa, 0.5 deg grid llipa)
  Derived:  permafrost_grid_0.5deg.csv (made from llipa.byte plus the permaice.dbf legend)
  Access:   anonymous FTP, no registration

4. permafrost_esa_cci_2023/  -  Permafrost extent 2023 (ESA CCI)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Dataset:  ESA Permafrost CCI, L4 permafrost extent (PFR), MODIS LST + CryoGrid, AREA4, v05.0, year 2023
  File:     ESACCI-PERMAFROST-L4-PFR-MODISLST_CRYOGRID-AREA4_PP-2023-fv05.0.nc (NetCDF, 12.4 MB)
  Variables: PFR (permafrost area fraction, %), PFR_uncertainty (%), with lat/lon (WGS84, 0.01 deg, 25N-85N)
  Project:  http://cci.esa.int/Permafrost

  Download: https://data.cci.ceda.ac.uk/thredds/fileServer/esacci/permafrost/data/permafrost_extent/L4/area4/pp/v05.0/northern_hemisphere/2023/ESACCI-PERMAFROST-L4-PFR-MODISLST_CRYOGRID-AREA4_PP-2023-fv05.0.nc
  Access:   no registration
